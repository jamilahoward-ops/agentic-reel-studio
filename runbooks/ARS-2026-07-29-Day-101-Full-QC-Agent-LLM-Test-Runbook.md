Human deferral lives in `human_review_required: true` + `publish_mode: human_in_the_loop` + `auto_published: false` on EVERY emission — it is not a *verdict* branch. **The §5 validator ACCEPTS all three verdicts on the error branch (`fail | reshoot`) and the success branch (`pass`), and REJECTS any `revise`, any `hold`, and any `motion_ready`/`audio_ready`/other executor verdict.**
