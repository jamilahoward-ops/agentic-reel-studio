## 1. MISSION / FIRST PRINCIPLE

> REINVENTED is not a rebrand. It's a reconstruction. We are not teaching women to look different — we are teaching them to build different.

Every reel must serve that sentence. If a reel cannot be traced back to "reconstruction, not rebrand," it doesn't ship.

## 2. AUDIENCE TARGET (who the reel is for)

**Primary:** Women 35–55 operating at founder / COO / senior-director level. High performers, high mental load, low systemization. Already credentialed — not looking for motivation. Looking for architecture.

**Psychographic:**
- Built their career on being *the one who handles it* — now that instinct is the bottleneck
- Income-strong, time-starved, identity-thin
- Tired of "boss babe" positioning; want operator-grade thinking
- Reads LinkedIn + Substack, listens to Diary of a CEO / Dan Martell / The Knowledge Project
- Suspicious of fluff; rewards specificity and proof
- Emotional core: quietly terrified they've outgrown the life they built

**What they are NOT:**
- Beginner entrepreneurs
- Motivational-content consumers
- Mindset-only audiences
- Women who want permission — they want blueprint

## 5. VOICE DNA — TONE RULES

**Cadence:**
- Short declarative sentences. Often 3–7 words.
- Two-part inversions: "This is not X. It is Y."
- Triplets: "We build. We delegate. We elevate."
- Direct second-person address: "You were taught to be dependable."

**Pacing patterns (reel-specific):**
- Hook: one clean sentence that reframes a known concept. 6–10 words.
- Body: pattern → break → pattern. Name the rule, break the rule, name the new rule.
- CTA: a question the viewer answers *to themselves* — never "link in bio / buy now."

**Mandatory voice markers (at least 2 per reel):**
- "On purpose"
- "Operator" (as noun describing the viewer)
- "System" (used as verb or noun)
- "Build" (preferred over "create")
- "The [thing] your [X] confesses" construction
- "Not a mindset problem. A [specific type] problem."
- Named identity archetypes: The Fixer. The Proof-Giver. The Glue.

**Pacing caveat:** one emotional beat per reel. Not two. If the reel has TWO "sit with this" moments, cut one.

## 6. BANNED LANGUAGE (hard no — QC auto-rejects)

### Banned vocabulary
- "Babe," "boss babe," "girl boss," "she-EO," "mompreneur"
- "Hustle," "grind," "rise and grind," "no days off"
- "Manifest" as verb, "vibes," "energy" (as a standalone claim)
- "Just believe in yourself," "you got this," "queen"
- "Disruptive," "game-changing," "next-level," "10x" (generic usage)
- "Genuinely," "honestly," "to be honest," "straightforward" *(per Jamila's global preference)*
- "Synergy," "circle back," "leverage" (as verb), "unpack"

### Banned moves
- Emojis in captions unless already in a named series template (MOS, Authority)
- Rhetorical questions stacked > 2 in a row
- "Listen…" / "Let me tell you…" as openers
- "As a woman who has…" or any credential-parading opener
- Motivational abstractions without an operating move attached
- Naming specific dollar amounts from Holdco Group, SLN, 4821 Main, 6315, NPOX
- References to Principal, Holdco Group, or any HC entity
- Mentions of internal tools: Claude, Cowork, Marblism, Lovable, GHL, Notion (by name)

### Banned postures
- Asking for permission
- Softening with "kind of" / "just" / "maybe"
- Qualifying authority claims ("I'm not an expert but…")
- Over-explaining — if she'd say it in one sentence, the reel says it in one sentence

## 7. VISUAL IDENTITY

**Aesthetic anchor:** Alux-style architectural minimalism. Think *Monocle × Kinfolk × Architectural Digest* in motion.

**Color palette:**
- Primary: deep ink black (#0E0E10)
- Secondary: warm ivory / parchment (#F5F0E6)
- Accent: antique gold (#C9A664)
- Forbidden: pink gradients, neon, pastel "feminine" palettes. Exception: one seasonal black-and-rose drop tolerated if overall series stays at <5%.

**Typography:**
- Display: a serif with architectural bones (Playfair / Canela / Tenor Sans) — used for pillar lines and final hold frame only
- Body/Caption: clean grotesque sans (Inter / GT Pressura / Neue Haas Grotesk)
- NEVER: script fonts, hand-lettered, decorative

**Frame language (Alux-style shot vocabulary):**
1. Held-object close-up (paper, pen, glass, cup — object stands for discipline)
2. Architectural interior (doorway, staircase, window — space as metaphor)
3. Slow push-in on face, ¾ angle, eyes off-camera then on
4. Text-only hold with serif pillar line
5. Hand-to-keyboard / hand-to-paper work shot
6. Exterior architectural detail (building facade, shadow, fabric texture)

**Motion:**
- Slow push-ins or static holds — NEVER whip-pans or shake
- Zero jump-cut comedy edits
- One text reveal per 8 seconds max
- No "trending" meme transitions

**Lower-thirds / supers:**
- Lowercase sans, left-aligned, subtle underline
- NEVER all-caps screamers

**Thumbnail rule:** Every reel ends on a text-only hold frame with one sentence (usually a Truth echo) in serif over ivory. That frame doubles as the thumbnail.

## 9. HOOK LIBRARY (ARS Brief Agent draws from / inherits pattern)

Rotate hook patterns; never repeat exact wording within a 30-reel window.

**Pattern A — Inversion:**
- "Your mission statement lies. Your calendar doesn't."
- "This isn't a mindset problem. It's an allocation problem."

**Pattern B — Named identity archetype:**
- "The Fixer built your career. She'll also cap it."
- "The Proof-Giver doesn't lead. She auditions."

**Pattern C — Uncomfortable truth:**
- "You are not burned out from the work. You are burned out from [specific frame]."
- "Operators don't talk about priorities. They schedule them."

**Pattern D — Reframing the common tool:**
- "AI didn't make me faster. It made my chaos visible."
- "Delegation isn't giving work away. It's choosing which identity to retire."

**Pattern E — Rule statement:**
- "Twice = systemize. Three times means you're already late."
- "If it's not on the calendar, it's not a priority. It's a wish."

---

## 10. CTA LIBRARY

Every CTA is a question the viewer answers INTERNALLY — never a sales ask.

- "Open your calendar. If someone had only this week to judge what you value, what would they say?"
- "Name the identity your role actually requires. Out loud. Today."
- "What are you doing twice this week that should be a system by Friday?"
- "Comment OPERATOR if this hit — I'll send the audit worksheet." *(only ~1 in 6 reels; saves conversion pull)*
- "The old you got you here. Who does the next version need to be?"

Rule: sales CTAs (link in bio, book a call, join the cohort) are banned at reel-level. They live in the caption, after a full scroll, never the reel itself.

## 11. CAPTION SPEC

- Length: 80–180 words
- Structure: first line echoes hook → 3–5 short body lines → one pillar-line flag → hashtag block
- First line is a scroll-stop — no "Hey guys!" openings
- Em-dashes allowed; one per caption max
- No emojis in caption body. Hashtag block = allowed lowercase
- Hashtag set (fixed 4–6):
  - #REINVENTED #MondayOperatorSeries #OperatorMindset #WomenInLeadership #IdentityReconstruction
  - Rotate in one of: #MagnetismOverAlgorithm #AIAsInfrastructure #ElevationAsDiscipline (pillar match)

## 14. EDGE CASES (how agents handle ambiguity)

**Case 1:** Topic aligns with TWO pillars equally → Route primary = the one with fewer appearances in last 10 reels. Secondary = the other.

**Case 2:** Jamila's raw audio came in off-brand (fatigue, distraction, "hustle" vocabulary slipped) → Editor strips, Script Agent rewrites in voice, Twin Drop replaces. NEVER ship raw-off-brand.

**Case 3:** Trending audio is irresistible → Reject. Audio ban is hard.

**Case 4:** Comment-spike suggestion ("do a reel on [topic]") → Strategy Agent evaluates against pillars before writing brief. Not every audience request earns a reel.

**Case 5:** Authority Piece contains a quote too long for reel → Pick the single sharpest sentence. The reel teases, the article delivers.

**Case 6:** Topic touches on Jamila's prior corporate role → Reject for REINVENTED. That's the firewall.

1. This brand profile (static until Saturday refresh)

- Brief JSON: { pillar_primary, pillar_secondary, hook_pattern, truth_echo_target, mode (A/B/C), series_template, cta_style, estimated_duration_sec, production_notes }
